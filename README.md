## 🧒 What is this? (explained like you're 10)

Imagine a magic notebook:

- You **write** "a lighthouse on a cliff at sunrise" → it **draws a moving video** for you.
- You give it **two photos** (the start and the end) → it makes the video travel from one to the other.
- It has a **video editor** (like a tiny movie studio) where you cut, add text, add music, and press **Export**.
- It has a **chat helper** that can write PDFs and Word files, make pictures, and even **rewrite your resume for a job** so a computer scanner (ATS) likes it more.
- It can make a **picture dance** by copying the moves of a person in a video.

It all runs **on your own computer**. No account. No monthly fee.

## 📥 Install — step by step

### Step 1 — Download
1. Click the big green **Download** button above.
2. Wait for the file `VKEditor-1.2.12-portable.exe` (about 85 MB) to finish.

### Step 2 — Put it in its own folder
1. Make a new folder, for example `F:\VKEditor` (any drive is fine).
2. Move the `.exe` file into that folder.

> 💡 **Why a folder?** The app saves your videos, settings and keys in a folder called `LTXStudioData` **next to the exe**. Keep it there and never delete it, or you lose your work.

### Step 3 — Open it
1. **Double-click** `VKEditor-1.2.12-portable.exe`.
2. Windows may show a blue box: **"Windows protected your PC"**. This is normal for new apps from independent makers.
   - Click **More info**
   - Click **Run anyway**

### Step 4 — Wait for the first-time setup (once only)
- A window shows ticks, the download **speed** and **time left**.
- It can take **10–40 minutes**. Keep the PC on and connected to the internet.
- If it stops or you close it, just open it again. **It continues where it stopped.**

### Step 5 — Press "Install everything"
On the Welcome screen press **Install everything (one click)**. The app looks at your PC and picks the best video model for it, then downloads it.

🎉 **Done!** A short tour shows you around. Press **Skip** if you don't want it.

## 🎥 Make your first video

1. Click **Make video** (top left).
2. Choose how to start:
   - **Text** – just describe what you want.
   - **Picture** – start from your photo. You can also add an **End picture** (the video finishes on it).
   - **Continue** – carry on from a video you already made.
3. Type what should happen, or type a few words and press **Improve prompt**. Example: *"a lighthouse on a cliff at sunrise, slow camera push-in"*.
4. Pick the shape: **16:9** (YouTube), **9:16** (Shorts / Reels), **1:1** (square), **4:5** (Instagram feed).
5. Look at the **estimate** under the button (for example *"about 6 minutes on your RTX 4050"*). If it says *too long*, choose a smaller size or a shorter clip.
6. Press **Generate**. You see a progress bar, the time passed, the time left and the live **CPU / GPU / memory** meters.
7. Made a mistake? Press the round **×** on a picture to remove it, or **Upload another**.

> ⏱ **How long does it take?** On a 6 GB laptop card with the graphics card switched on, a short clip takes a few minutes. If it takes much longer, open **Settings → This PC**: it must say your graphics card is working. Videos are saved in the **Library** automatically.

## ✂️ Edit your video

1. Click **Editor**.
2. Drag your videos, pictures or music into the window (or press **Add media**).
3. Use the tools: **Split**, **Duplicate**, **Delete**, **Add text**, **Draw**.
4. Talk to the AI at the bottom: *"make it vertical"*, *"add a fade"*, *"put the title My Trip"*. Press **Ctrl + Z** to undo anything.
5. Not sure which AI to connect? Press **Best AI for my PC** in the chat panel.
6. Press the green **Export** button and choose where to save the file. 📁

## 🕺 Motion control (make a picture copy a dance or move)

Add a **Character picture** (one person, head to toe) and a **Motion video** (3–30 seconds of one person moving). Then pick an engine:

| Engine | Quality | Cost | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Realistic (Kling, online)** | Looks like a real video | Pay per video on your fal.ai account | A fal.ai key |
| **Quick preview (free, on this PC)** | Rough "puppet" preview: your picture is bent to follow the body movement. Clothes and face are stretched, not re-drawn | Free | Nothing. The first use downloads a small 6 MB body-finder file |

**Using Kling (fal.ai):**
1. Make an account at [fal.ai](https://fal.ai), add a little credit, then open **Dashboard → Keys → Create key**.
2. Copy the **real** key. It looks like `abc123…:def456…`. (Not the words "paste your key".)
3. In the app: **Motion control** → paste it → **Save key** → **Generate**.

**Using the free Quick preview:** choose *Quick preview*, add the two files, press **Generate**. It uses Google's MediaPipe to find the body. If it says it cannot download the body-finder file, download it once from the link in the message and put it in `LTXStudioData\models\pose`.

## 🤖 AI Assistant (the chat helper)

Click **AI Assistant** in the left menu. A second window opens.

**First, tell it which AI brain to use** (once): open **Settings** inside the assistant. Not sure? Press **Help me choose the best AI for my PC**. The easy free choices are **Ollama** (runs on your PC) or a free online key (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter). For the best writing you can add a paid **Claude** key.

What you can do:

| You want to… | Type or click |
| --- | --- |
| Make a PDF, slides, Word, CSV or Excel file | `/pdf`, `/ppt`, `/doc`, `/csv`, `/xlsx` then say what you want |
| Make a picture from words | **Images**. Your short idea is automatically expanded into a detailed prompt |
| Check your resume score | **Resume ATS** and attach your resume |
| **Rewrite your resume for a job** | Attach your resume **and** the job description, then type: *"Tailor my resume to this JD with a high ATS score"* |
| Compare two files | **Compare files** |
| Understand a spreadsheet | **Analyse file** |

### 📄 Resume tailoring in 4 steps
1. Click the **+** button and attach your resume (PDF, Word, text or CSV).
2. Attach the job description too (or paste it in the message).
3. Type: *Tailor my resume to this JD with a high ATS score* and press send.
4. You get a **new PDF** and your score **before → after**. It only uses facts from your own resume, and it lists job words it could not add honestly. **Always read it before sending it to a company.**

## 🔄 Updating to a new version

1. Download the new `.exe` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/karthikeyan12345678/offline-Local-AI-Video-Editor/releases/latest).
2. Put it in the **same folder** as the old one (next to `LTXStudioData`).
3. Run the new one. Your videos, settings and keys stay.

Small parts (captions, MediaPipe, OpenCV…) can also be refreshed in **Settings → Updates** without a new download.

## 🆘 Something is wrong?

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Windows protected your PC" | More info → **Run anyway** |
| Setup stuck | Close the app and open it again — it continues |
| Video takes hours | Open **Settings → This PC**. If the graphics card is not used, update your NVIDIA driver and use the graphics card fix. Choose a smaller size; the estimate under Generate shows the time |
| "Expected all tensors to be on the same device" | Fixed in 1.2.12. Update the app |
| fal.ai "did not accept the key" | You pasted example text or a wrong key. Create a key at fal.ai → Dashboard → Keys and paste the real one |
| Motion Quick preview says no person found | Use a clear picture of one person, head to toe, and a video where one person is fully visible |
| Generate button is grey | The line under it tells you why (for example models still downloading or the video is too long) |
| Drag and drop does nothing | Don't run the app "as administrator" |
| Lost videos | Look in the `LTXStudioData` folder next to the exe |
| Still stuck | Open an [Issue](https://github.com/karthikeyan12345678/offline-Local-AI-Video-Editor/issues) and attach a screenshot (hide any keys first) |

## 🔐 Privacy and your keys

- Your videos, pictures and files stay **on your computer**.
- Keys you paste (fal.ai, Claude, OpenAI, Groq…) are saved **only inside your `LTXStudioData` folder** on your PC and sent only to that service. They are **never** inside the app or this repository.
- ⚠️ **Never** paste a real key into GitHub, an Issue, a README, a screenshot or a chat. If a key was ever shared, delete it on the service's website and create a new one.
- If you fork this project, keep `LTXStudioData/` out of git (the included `.gitignore` already does this).
- Online helpers (free picture service, online AI chat) only get what you send them.
